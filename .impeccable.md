# Design Context

Audience: boutique hotel staff and guests, small travel businesses, exploring Athens.
Use: enter cultural tastes, choose dining and attraction preferences, review sourced recommendations, compare a generic LLM, edit preferences.
Tone: creator-mandated calm, polished, modern Geist interface. White/neutral surfaces, generous whitespace, restrained #006bff interactions. Geist Sans, no orange, gradients or emoji. Explicit limitations and honest unavailable states.
