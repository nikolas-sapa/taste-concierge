# Practical fixture sources

`athens.json` contains 39 independent Wikidata records: 4 dining fixtures and 35 attractions. The original 20 published records remain unchanged at the record-value level, including null and conflicting coordinates. Nineteen eligible museum records were appended from `outputs/practical-candidates-expanded.json`; each retains its source claims, revisions, dates, labels, aliases, websites, type proof, and provenance. The app maps source category `museum` to `attractions` and uses CC0 for the structured Wikidata facts.

Museum class verification is documented in [`museum-type-evidence.json`](museum-type-evidence.json), copied verbatim from the expanded candidate pool's class evidence. Candidate records include direct P31=Q33506 or source-verified P31/P279 paths to Q33506. The retained class statement IDs and Wikidata claim URLs identify the supported edges. Only eligible records with a direct Athens (Q1524) or Athens Municipality (Q1224979) P131 claim, one unambiguous normal Earth P625 coordinate, and source-reported distance within 6 km were appended. The pool contains 28 eligible museums; 9 were already represented, so 19 unique records were added.

The retained Wikipedia category/page URLs are discovery and selection provenance only; no article prose is copied or used as factual support for fixture fields. `athens-reference.json` preserves the source-reported Athens reference coordinate with revision, precision, retrieval date, and CC0 attribution. It is used only for the straight-line radius calculation, not as a verified city centroid, boundary, or walking origin.

No Qloo identifiers, responses, ranks, matches, or private linkage are included. These independent source facts do not establish Qloo identity or a bound recommendation. A fixture remains pending binding until an exact backend-supplied `InternalCandidate.fixtureId` match is established; matching evidence remains a separate gate. No Qloo linkage is inferred from names, aliases, coordinates, or proximity.

Every source-reported location and class claim describes the independent Wikidata statement only. Coordinates and category do not establish current operation, business hours, prices, menu, accessibility, availability, safety, or booking facts. Missing and conflicting data remain unknown.
