Additions were limited to records with a direct P31 claim to Q33506, one normal terrestrial P625 coordinate matching the sole original coordinate, direct P131 membership in Athens (Q1524) or Athens Municipality (Q1224979), and a great-circle distance no greater than 6 km from the source-reported Athens reference coordinate in `athens-reference.json`. The existing Byzantine and Christian Museum record (Q1018775) already covered one otherwise eligible candidate, so it was not duplicated. Six additions produce the declared total of 20 unique fixture IDs, 4 dining and 16 attractions.

The retained Wikipedia category/page URLs are discovery and selection provenance only; no article prose is copied or used as factual support for the app category. No records outside these six additions were incorporated from the expanded candidate pool.

`athens-reference.json` preserves the single non-deprecated Wikidata P625 coordinate statement for Q1524 with source revision, precision, retrieval date, and CC0 attribution. It is used only as a source-reported straight-line radius reference point. It is not a verified city centroid, boundary, or walking origin.

No Qloo identifiers, responses, ranks, matches, or private linkage are included. These independent source facts do not establish Qloo identity. A fixture without an exact backend-supplied `InternalCandidate.fixtureId` match is ineligible; any matching/binding evidence remains a separate gate. No Qloo linkage is inferred from names, aliases, coordinates, or proximity.

Every source-reported location and class claim describes the independent Wikidata statement only. Coordinates and category do not establish current operation, business hours, prices, menu, accessibility, availability, safety, or booking facts. Missing and conflicting data remain unknown.
